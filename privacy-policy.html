Riven Audio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Riven Audio ("the app") is a control application for the Riven Audio Soundscape Reprojection Processor (SRP), a hardware audio device connected via USB. This policy describes what data the app does and does not collect.

Summary

The app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. It does not have user accounts, does not use analytics or advertising services, and does not transmit any information that identifies you or your device to us or to any third party.

What the app does

  • USB communication with your SRP device. The app exchanges control data (settings such as volume, input source, and audio processing options) directly with a connected SRP over a wired USB connection. This communication stays local between your phone or tablet and your device — it is never sent anywhere else.
  • A single network request to check for updates. On launch, the app makes one unauthenticated, public request to fetch a version-information file hosted on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). This request does not include any information about you or your device beyond what any web request inherently includes at the network level (such as your IP address, which is handled by GitHub's own infrastructure, not by us — see GitHub's own privacy statement for how they handle that). The app does not send your settings, usage, or any other data as part of this request.

What the app does not do

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking
  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files
  • No data shared with third parties

Permissions

The app requests Internet access (to check for updates, as described above) and USB device access (to communicate with a connected SRP). It requests no other permissions.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, since it does not collect data at all.

Changes to this policy
